# Harrington platform audit — 27 September 2026

**Purpose.** A full inventory of what Harrington is today, how complete each part is,
and what stands between the current build and a family using it every day from next
week. Written from the angle of a self-hosted education platform (learner model,
curriculum, instruction, assessment, evidence, analytics, engagement, collaboration,
operations) even though the immediate customer is one family with three children.

**Method.** Every source file was read (9.6k lines of browser JS, the 400-line server,
tests, docs, CI and Docker config). The test suite was run. A synthetic family of three
(ages 3, 6 and 9) was created in a headless Chromium walkthrough of every route, the
child view, a mobile viewport and every AI-backed button, with console and network
errors captured. The Marble taxonomy was pulled to measure the curriculum and the
cold-start problem. Linear (team HAR) and the open GitHub pull requests were reviewed.
Audited at commit `b67b5f2` (main, includes this morning's merge of PR #7).

---

## 1. TL;DR

Harrington is a **well-built, honest, single-family learning platform in a "Sprout"
state**: the skeleton is solid, the parent-facing curriculum map and daily rhythm are
real, and the privacy posture is better than the upstream it forked from. It is also a
platform where **roughly half of the visible buttons lead to a dead end by default**,
because the whole instruction and assessment pipeline (lessons, printables, tests,
challenges, recall cards, coaching, insights) is generated by an AI provider that is
intentionally not configured.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does it start, persist and render cleanly? | Yes. 40/40 tests pass. Zero console errors across every route with three learners. Mobile layout has no horizontal overflow. |
| Can a parent use it next week without AI? | Yes for: learner profiles, the curriculum map, daily literacy/numeracy choices, manual mastery marking, notes and voice recordings, the calendar, and progress views. |
| What does not work without AI? | Lessons, print-and-go sheets, all mastery tests (topic, section, subject), challenges, recall cards, spaced practice (starved), activity instructions, AI coaching, progress reviews, adaptivity, certificates, most badges. |
| What is actually broken (not just gated)? | Recall scheduling (cards become unloadable after first grading), the recorder leaves the microphone on if the modal is dismissed with Escape or a backdrop tap, a challenge timer keeps running after dismissal and later records a phantom result, "Review answers → Back" re-saves a test and re-awards XP, and the typed-answer grader accepts any substring. |
| Does the child view keep scores hidden? | Mostly. The overlay itself shows only plants and words, but launching a test, challenge or recall session from it shows percentages, an answer key and "+25 XP / Level up!" popups, and the "Grown-ups" button has no PIN. |
| Biggest product gap for a real 9-year-old? | Placement. The app assumes zero prior mastery. For a 9-year-old, 862 of 1,590 topics sit below their age band and the only way to mark them is one hidden button per topic. |
| Biggest data risk? | Whole-state, last-writer-wins persistence with no version check, no export, no backup, and silent save failures. Two devices open at once will overwrite each other. |

## 2. What the platform is

**Stack.** Static HTML + vanilla ES modules (no framework, no bundler), a locally built
Tailwind stylesheet with the "Storybook Meadow" theme, vendored Lucide icons and two
self-hosted variable fonts. A dependency-free Node server (`server.mjs`) serves the app
and five APIs: health, taxonomy proxy/cache, family state, lesson cache, audio blobs, and
an OpenAI-compatible AI proxy. Docker and Compose are present (loopback-only publish).
GitHub Actions runs `npm test` on push and PR.

**Data model.** One JSON document per family at `/api/state` (students, progress, records,
tests, plan, challenges, adaptations, suggestions, notifications, curriculum snapshot,
recall, practice, activity, game, daily, graphView). Lesson/printable/recall caches are
one file per key under `data/private/lessons/`. Recordings are blobs under
`data/private/audio/`. The taxonomy is cached once under `data/private/taxonomy/`.

## 3. Verified in this audit

**Automated.** `npm test` → 40 passed, 0 failed (build + node test runner).

**Server.** `/api/health` reports `mode: self-hosted`, `aiConfigured: false`. First-run
taxonomy download targets jsDelivr; in this sandbox that host is blocked, which reproduced
the real failure mode: the browser shows only "Could not load the curriculum data." The
override `HARRINGTON_TAXONOMY_UPSTREAM` exists but is not surfaced in that error. After
seeding the cache from GitHub, health reported `taxonomyCached: true`.

**Browser walkthrough (headless Chromium, 1280×900 and 390×844).**

- Onboarding created "Sample Nine" (2017); two more learners were added from the switcher.
  All three survived a reload and were present in `/api/state`.
- Dashboard, Calendar, Map (visual world map, skill tree, list drill-down), Records,
  Insights, a topic page, the Guide modal and the child view all rendered. **Zero console
  or page errors** across the run other than the expected 404s (empty lesson cache) and
  503s (AI unconfigured).
- The 390 px viewport has no horizontal overflow.
- Today's path for the 9-year-old offered two literacy options (Reading Comprehension,
  Writing Composition) and two numeracy options (Place Value, Addition & Subtraction),
  plus a "Refresher quiz" on The K-Pg Extinction Event, a topic the learner has never seen.
- The 3-year-old received no daily choices and a "gentle review day" (the taxonomy starts
  at age 4 and the app clamps to 5).
- AI buttons on the topic page: **Open full lesson** says "AI is not configured." (the
  only honest one); **Print & go** says "Couldn't prepare the materials right now";
  **Practice recall** says "Couldn't load recall cards"; **Take topic mastery test** opens
  an intro that promises a test and fails on "Create the test".
- Child view overlay: no `%`, `XP` or `Level` strings in the overlay itself. But **Plant
  something new** opened the mastery-test modal, which reads "Needs 90% or more correct",
  and **Memory walk** opened the failing recall modal.

## 5. Cross-cutting risks

**Privacy and safety**
- Child names and parent notes are interpolated into prompts by discussion analysis, progress review and the (unmounted) Helper, despite the README/SECURITY policy and the guard tests, which only cover lesson/explain prompts. Will leak the moment any endpoint is configured.
- Live transcription is cloud speech-to-text by the browser vendor. Either label it or default it off.
- `index.html` posts errors to any parent frame.
- No PIN between the child view and the parent view.

**Data integrity**
- Last-writer-wins whole-blob persistence, no unload flush, silent failures, 5 MB cap after which every save fails silently. Audio is uploaded before the record is persisted, so a failed state save orphans the file while the parent saw "Recording saved".
- No export/import/backup.
- `removeStudent` orphans most per-learner keys and never deletes that learner's audio.

**Correctness (see §4 for file references)**
- Mic left running on modal dismissal; challenge timer outlives its modal; duplicate test results and XP; substring grading; recall `topicId` wipe; permanent caching of empty printables and activity details; infinite spinner on lesson regeneration.

**Unescaped HTML (stored self-XSS, third-party taxonomy text)**
- `topic.js` and `timeline.js` escape nothing (including parent-entered names and record titles); `calendar.js`, `masterytest.js`, `challenge.js`, `recall.js`, the graph List mode and several headers inject names or taxonomy strings raw. `ui.js` already exports a complete `esc()`; five views ship their own quote-less copies. Low severity today (no auth, single family), but the taxonomy is downloaded from a CDN.

**Honesty of copy**
- Welcome tour and Guide promise lessons "one tap away", AI-verified tests and automatic curriculum updates; the nav they describe (Timeline) no longer exists; the Guide is unreachable on mobile. `src/docs/GUIDE.md` is not linked from anywhere. `CHANGELOG.md` is Homestead's. The PR template asks contributors to confirm they "signed in". The GitHub repository description still begins "Homestead turns the open Marble Skill Taxonomy…".

**Dead code worth pruning**
- `views/assistant.js`, `views/coop.js`, `views/daysheet.js` (unreachable); `setTimelineSubject`, `sectionUnlocked`, `topicsRemaining`, `trackDayFor`, `pageHeader`, `allPrereqs`, `AGES`, section-graph helpers in `graph.js` used only by tests; unused imports in `timeline.js`, `calendar.js`, `game.js`, `topic.js`.

## 7. Recommended next cycle: "ready for Monday"

> **Superseded on 1 October 2026.** The re-ranked list, with what shipped and what is
> in flight, is in [2026-10-01-cycle-1-status.md](2026-10-01-cycle-1-status.md) §6.

### Decisions to make first

1. **AI provider for week one.** Run Ollama (or a vendor endpoint) now, or run without AI
   and make the non-AI path first-class? Recommendation: ship week one **without AI** and
   make the app honest about it; stand up Ollama in parallel as a week-two experiment on
   lessons only. Small local models frequently fail the strict JSON the test generator
   demands, so tests should not be the first thing tried.
2. **Devices.** Laptop-only (works today) or tablets over the LAN (needs a host bind, some
   authentication, and HTTPS for the microphone)? Recommendation: laptop for week one; LAN
   plus a shared secret and TLS in cycle two.
3. **Gamification.** Keep XP/levels/badges as parent-only, or remove them? They are the
   main source of child-view leaks. Recommendation: suppress every celebration and number
   while the child view is open, keep the data.
4. **PR #5.** See Appendix A.

### Tier 0 — before first real use (estimated 2–3 days)

1. **Honest no-AI mode.** Read `/api/health` once at boot; hide or badge every AI-gated
   button when `aiConfigured` is false and show one consistent message with a link to the
   README section. Lesson already does this; generalise it.
2. **Promote manual mastery and add placement.** Make the status buttons first-class on
   the topic page and quest log, and add "mark everything in this domain / subject below
   age N as mastered" with a confirmation. This single feature turns the map, rings and
   daily choices from empty to useful for the 6- and 9-year-old.
3. **Child view containment.** PIN on "Grown-ups"; suppress `award()` popups, percentages
   and answer keys while the overlay is open; make "Plant something new" and the daily
   picks open something a child can do (the quick check, evidence list and activities)
   rather than a 90 % test.
4. **Fix `openModal` so backdrop and Escape call the overridable close.** One change in
   `ui.js` fixes the microphone-left-on bug and the phantom challenge result. Add a
   confirmation before abandoning a recording or a test in progress.
5. **Data safety.** Export/import of the family JSON (and a documented copy of
   `data/private/`); `beforeunload` flush with `sendBeacon`; a version number on the state
   document with `If-Match` on PUT so a stale tab reloads instead of overwriting; a toast
   on save failure.
6. **Small correctness fixes.** Recall `topicId` (one line); duplicate result on "Back to
   result"; substring grading; empty-test guard; delete the `index.html` telemetry shim;
   route all `innerHTML` interpolation of names, record fields and taxonomy text through
   `ui.esc()`.
7. **Copy honesty.** Rewrite the tour, Guide and onboarding text to describe what works
   today; drop "use a sample name" once export exists; remove Timeline references; add
   Guide to the mobile nav; retire `CHANGELOG.md` or start a Harrington one; fix the PR
   template and the GitHub repository description.

### Tier 1 — first two weeks of use

- **Non-AI assessment path.** The taxonomy already ships `evidence[]` and an
  `assessmentPrompt` per topic. An "evidence checklist" flow (parent ticks the evidence
  seen, optional note or recording, saves a test-shaped result) gives sections and
  subjects a way to pass, feeds the practice queue, and matches the POC's preference for
  observed evidence over quizzes.
- **Calendar realism.** Holidays and term breaks; an on-ramp for topics below the current
  age; refreshers only from mastered topics; remove the dead button; "Extra practice"
  opens practice.
- **Interest-led invitations** (the rest of HAR-4) on top of `daily.js`, with the
  coverage-claim rule enforced at record time.
- **Learner editing** (name, birth month/year, colour) and a `removeStudent` that cleans
  every key and deletes audio.
- **Evidence attachments** (photos) stored as blobs next to audio, never base64 in the
  state document.
- **Notifications**: remove the bell or give it a real source (for example daily choices
  not picked, evidence not recorded this week).
- **Tests** for the store, mastery, scheduler and the modal-close contract.

## Appendix A — PR #5 assessment (HAR-4 daily parent surface)

Branch `cursor/har-4-daily-parent-surface-2438` (head `a09b132`, three commits, +1,086 /
−16) against merge base `5dc9ca8`. Main has since moved five commits, including a
430-line rewrite of the dashboard and the `daily.js` pick-one feature.

**What PR #5 adds.**
- Interest chips plus free text per learner, and a per-day queue of exactly three
  invitations (make / research / practice) built from lexical matching of the interest
  against topic names and descriptions. Only "bird shelter" has real routing logic; every
  other interest differs by string templating. A "Bird-shelter demo" button and a default
  "Bird shelter" chip ship for every new learner.
- Ready-now literacy/numeracy panels (three per lane, recomputed on each render), a
  "why locked" path for blocked topics, and a read-only "review due" box.
- Evidence capture extensions: records and recordings can be linked to an invitation,
  carry an opt-in "mark curriculum coverage" flag, and accept up to two photo/file
  attachments stored **as base64 inside the state document** (300 KB per file, with a
  4.5 MB pre-save estimate). It also escapes several previously raw fields.
- Six pure tests; one is a brittle regex over source text.

**Against the HAR-4 acceptance criteria.** Main now satisfies "ready-now literacy and
numeracy options" better than the PR (two per lane, domain-diverse, frozen per day, parent
and child views) and most of "plain-language record after an activity". Only PR #5
addresses "interest → meaningfully different invitations" (thinly), "hard prerequisites
that block a claim are shown to the parent", and "a choice is not labelled coverage
unless evidence supports it" (as an explicit mechanism).

**Conflicts (`git merge-tree`).** `tailwind.css` (regenerate), `store.js` (textually a
comment, semantically a collision: both sides use `state.daily[studentId]` with
incompatible shapes and main's 14-day prune would delete the PR's keys), `dashboard.js`
(auto-merges into a page with two competing literacy/numeracy surfaces in the
pre-Meadow palette), `topic.js` (import line). Shell nav would rename Dashboard to
"Daily".

**Risks.** Base64 attachments inside a 5 MB single document: roughly five or six photo
records before every later save fails silently. Random invitation ids orphan linked
records whenever the queue regenerates. Duplicate reimplementations of `isUnlocked` and
the lane domains. No Puter or upstream leftovers.

**Recommendation: close PR #5 as a PR and salvage three pieces onto main's `daily.js`**
(about one to two days):
1. The record/recorder options (`invitation`, `coverageTopicIds`), the opt-in coverage
   checkbox, the `coverage` and `invitationId` record fields, the escaping fixes, and
   the evidence-summary gate. Drop the base64 attachments or redo them as a blob route
   like `/api/audio/`.
2. Blocked/why-locked reporting as a small extension of `laneOptions` returning
   `{ options, blocked }`, rendered as one line inside each pick-one stop, with the
   fixture tests moved into `tests/daily.test.mjs`.
3. Interest capture (chips and free text) under a non-colliding key such as
   `state.interests`, without the demo defaults, restyled to Meadow tokens, and with a
   real interest-to-domain map (or the AI route) behind the invitations.

Move HAR-4 back to In Progress with PR #5 referenced as the source of the salvaged
pieces. Merging as-is would take two to three hours and leave criterion one effectively
unmet while adding the risks above.
